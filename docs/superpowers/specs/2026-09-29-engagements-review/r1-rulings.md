| 1 | Guards must stay opt-in; Torque retired default global interception (Gemini P0, Codex P0-2) | ACCEPT (verified in Torque AGENTS.md and product rules: trust ladder, no mandatory gate) | Section 8 rewritten: guards only in opt-in protected mode per client; a host may turn protected mode on for the VM through managed settings |
